## What the run does
1. `sf_ncbi_fetch` (x2, pri and alt): reads the NCBI folder for GCA_062019780.1 and
   GCA_062019781.1 (`genomes/all/GCA/062/019/78{0,1}/`), downloads `*_genomic.fna.gz`,
   `*_assembly_report.txt` and `md5checksums.txt` to sf scratch, and checks both md5s against
   NCBI's; a mismatch is retried, then fails the job.
2. `sf_telomere_scan` (x2): takes assembly-report rows with Sequence-Role `assembled-molecule`
   and Assigned-Molecule-Location/Type `Chromosome` (pri: chr1-38, X, Y; alt: chr1-38; chrM
   excluded), checks each is in the FASTA at the report length, and scans both ends of each.
3. `sf_telomeres_publish` (x1): writes the Tier 2 run folder, with run_info.json, a sha256
   listing and `_MANIFEST.txt`. The manifest is the t2t bucket's header-only block of aligned
   `key: value` lines: `describes:` (the SPEC row), `source:` (the scratch folder), `dest:`,
   `job:` (this job's SLURM id), `scope: metric`, `finished:`, `objects:` and `size:` (the
   folder's other files), then `lane:`, `commit:`, `run_id:` and `spec_id:`. Per-file sha256s
   are in `sha256sums.txt`, not the manifest.

Method per chromosome end (`start` = first base, `end` = last base; full text in method.txt):
exact TTAGGG and CCCTAA counts in the terminal 1, 10 and 100 kb, and per orientation the
terminal array within 100 kb: exact copies chained across gaps of <= 18 bp, runs of >= 10
copies qualify, and the one nearest the end is reported as span bp, copies and distance from
the end. `expected_motif` is CCCTAA at the start and TTAGGG at the end. No present/absent
calls: every number is diagnostic-only until M03 and both humans.
