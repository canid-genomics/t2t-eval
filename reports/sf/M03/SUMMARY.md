An end counts as having an array when M02's expected-motif array there (CCCTAA at the start, TTAGGG at the end) has ≥10 copies and starts within 10 000 bp of the end (Jonah's proposal, agreed by Steven 2026-11-23, replacing 100 000 bp, M02's whole search window; any cutoff ≥3356 bp gives the same counts here); `status` is a fixed rule on the two ends and the gap count.
- **Numbers** (all from `reports/sf/M03/completeness.tsv`): 147 of 156 ends have an array, and the nine without one are the same nine as in the approved M02 summary (pri chr10 end, chr16 start, chr31 start; alt chr9 start, chr10 end, chr16 start, chr31 end, chr34 start, chr38 start). pri (40 rows): 36 `array_both_ends_no_gaps`, 1 `array_both_ends_with_gaps` (chr7), 3 `array_one_end` (chr10, chr16, chr31), 0 `no_array`. alt (38 rows): 31, 1 (chr7), 6 (chr9, chr10, chr16, chr31, chr34, chr38), 0. chr7 is the only chromosome with gaps, 2 per haplotype of 10000 bp each, all within the last 321 kb of chr7 (pri 200830–291286 bp from the end, alt 230344–320800 bp). chrX and chrY are in pri only.
- **Caveats:** verdict-safe for these counts only (PROVENANCE.md); a `status` is a rule on the M01/M02 numbers, not a call about the assembly, and any claim waits for both humans; M02's caveats carry over (exact matches only, only the terminal 100 kb searched, the array is the qualifying run nearest the end), as do M01's (a gap is a run of N in the FASTA; errors without Ns are not measured); 142 of the 147 arrays lie within 1 kb of the end and five lie 1234–3356 bp in (pri chr8 start; alt chr16 end, chr24 start, chr29 end, chr32 start), so they would not count at a 1 kb threshold; chrM, unlocalized and unplaced scaffolds are left out.
